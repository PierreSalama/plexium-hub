Privacy Policy

Plexium Hub for iOS · last updated 14 November 2026

Plexium Hub sends your data to one place only: the server you sign in to. There is no advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDK. Nothing is sold or shared.

How the app works

Plexium Hub is a client for a media server that someone runs, usually on a computer at home. The app talks to the server address you sign in to. Your library, your account, your history and your backups live on that server, and the person who runs it can see what is stored there. If you use a server run by the developer, the developer is that person.

What the app sends to your server

  • What you play: history, playback positions, lists and ratings, so the server can resume and recommend.
  • Backups you start: photos and videos, voice notes and contacts, only when you choose to back them up.
  • Problem reports you send: the screenshot, your message and an app log, only when you tap Send.
  • Device health: while you use the app, about every 30 seconds, your device's heat level, battery level, the app's own processor and memory use, and which screen is open, so the server's owner can keep the app from overheating phones. It is kept for 90 days.
  • App logs: errors and timings from the app, used to fix problems.

All of it is used only to make the app work, and none of it leaves that server.

What stays on your device

  • The server address you entered, and your sign-in session for it.
  • Media you chose to download for offline playback.
  • Your app settings, and playback positions while you are offline.

All of it is removed when you delete the app.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Local network: to reach your server directly when your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as it.
  • Photos: only if you choose to back photos or videos up, and only for the items you pick.
  • Contacts: only if you choose to back up your address book.
  • Microphone: only if you choose to record a voice note.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from Settings in the app; this removes your data from the server. You can also write to the address below.

Third parties

There are none. The app embeds no third-party analytics, advertising or attribution SDK, and it does not sell or share any information.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
